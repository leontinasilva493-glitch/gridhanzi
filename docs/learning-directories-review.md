# Radical and pinyin directories: review and link inventory

## Scope

Implemented in priority order: grid-paper titles, existing homepage grid link verification, radicals hub plus 30 families, pinyin hub plus 30 syllables, contextual worksheet handoff and internal links. The existing 150 stroke-order guides are retained. Pinterest and external directory submission are excluded by the user's latest instruction. No additional how-to-write, HSK-vocabulary, phrase, zodiac or name-generator pages are added.

The supplied monthly search volumes and difficulty values were not independently verified and are not published as facts. No claim of new impressions, changed landing-page rankings, or guaranteed indexing is made. Search performance needs a later GSC comparison; a successful deployment does not establish it.

- [Pinyin hub](https://gridhanzi.org/pinyin): 409 reference spellings, initials/finals/tone explanation, 30 reviewed syllable pages. Title is scoped to actual text guidance; no audio or universal four-tone word coverage is promised.

## Evidence and content boundaries

- [Unicode 17.0 CJKRadicals.txt](https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/ucd/CJKRadicals.txt): canonical 1–214 radical numbering and heading forms. Group counts are 6, 23, 31, 34, 23, 29, 20, 9, 11, 8, 6, 4, 4, 2, 1, 2, 1 for 1–17 strokes.
- [Make Me a Hanzi dictionary](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/master/dictionary.txt): reference readings and 9,574 character-to-radical classifications. The 30 family examples were checked against this mapping, including 亻/人, 氵/水, 纟/糸, 艹/艸 and 钅/金. Source notices and LGPL/Unicode licences are distributed with the derived lookup data.
- [Pinyin.info initials and finals](https://pinyin.info/rules/initials_finals.html): reference combinations, including uncommon spellings. Only 30 syllables have detailed lessons; the rest are clearly reference entries, not empty indexable pages.
- Contextual examples override incomplete dictionary defaults. In particular 切 qiē (cut), 地 dì (ground), 都 dū (capital), 几 jǐ (how many) and neutral 吗 ma are selected deliberately. [MOE 切](https://dict.concised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=24798&la=0&powerMode=0) and its [pinyin reference](https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/FileUpload/3692-16962/Documents/pinyinshouce.pdf) provide supplementary reading evidence.
- The 30 radical details have distinct form, meaning-limit and exercise paragraphs; the 30 pinyin details have distinct articulation, tone/context and exercise paragraphs. Automated checks verify 180 unique editorial sections, 60 distinct titles and 60 distinct descriptions.
- Kangxi heading counts are distinguished from component-form counts. The visible 月 shape does not automatically mean the moon radical; lookup uses the dataset's indexing classification. Rare headings are labelled reference names, not beginner vocabulary.
- Pinyin guidance distinguishes y/w spelling conventions, ü versus u, ui/iu/un contractions, contextual readings, neutral tone and third-tone behaviour. Empty tone slots are not filled with invented words.
- Existing component pages describe construction and semantic/phonetic contributions. New radical pages describe indexing families and a short writing exercise. Four existing semantic component pages and their corresponding radical pages link both ways instead of duplicating explanations.

## Handoff and indexing

`/generator?learning=radical:water` and `/generator?learning=pinyin:du` load reviewed entries with their pinyin and English senses intact. They do not call automatic enrichment. Character-standard conversion changes Hanzi only, retaining selected readings. Unknown, malformed or repeated lesson parameters return 404. Lesson parameter pages are noindex and excluded from the sitemap. Chinese variants of the new English learning pages remain noindex.

Homepage body links lead to both hubs; each hub links to its 30 details in server HTML. Detail pages return to the hub, link to published character guides or explicitly labelled lookup tools, and open the worksheet editor. Relevant existing character guides link to their radical or base-syllable lesson. All 62 new public pages are listed once in the sitemap; the stroke-order count stays 150.
