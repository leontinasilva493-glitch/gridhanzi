# Hanzi Gacha editorial review

Reviewed: 2026-11-15. Scope: each of the 132 displayed character readings, English glosses, contextual examples, and example readings below. Character readings show one chosen use; an example can have a contextual neutral tone or tone change. English glosses are teaching summaries, not official HSK definitions. The HSK flag is solely membership in the first two groups of `hsk-writing-characters.json` (2025 HSK 3.0 Levels 1–2 combined and Level 3 writing groups).

References: existing published character guides; `hsk-catalog.json` (community vocabulary and CC-CEDICT-derived glosses, see VOCABULARY-LICENSE.md); `hsk-2025-beginner-source.json` (the official 2025 Level 1–2 vocabulary and pinyin). Example reference is marked syllabus when the word occurs in the official source, catalogue when it occurs in the community catalogue, and contextual phrase when it is absent from both and was reviewed as a simple phrase or checked separately. Source membership alone does not verify the English sense or prove exam writing requirements. The syllabus is a pronunciation reference for examples present in its vocabulary table, not an official translation source.

Separately checked lexical examples: [玉石](https://www.yellowbridge.com/chinese/charsearch.php?zi=%E7%8E%89), [骑马](https://zdic.net/hans/%E9%AA%91%E9%A9%AC). Contextual phrases include tone changes such as 一个 yí ge and neutral syllables such as 儿子 érzi; these are not claims that the character has the same reading in isolation. 目光, 子女, 经过, 议论 and 快车 are present in the local community catalogue.
